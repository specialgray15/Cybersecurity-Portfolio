# Tusk Infostealer – Cyber Threat Intelligence Investigation

## Project Overview
- **Platform:** CyberDefenders
- **Category:** Cyber Threat Intelligence
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Completion Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Status:** Completed (9/9)

## Objective
Investigate a malware campaign targeting cryptocurrency users, identify indicators of compromise (IOCs), and analyze threat actor infrastructure and attack methods.

## Tools Used
- Kaspersky Threat Intelligence Portal
- Securelist
- Google OSINT Research

## Investigation Methodology
1. Examined the provided MD5 hash.
2. Conducted a hash lookup to identify malware characteristics.
3. Pivoted from MD5 to SHA256 to locate relevant threat intelligence reporting.
4. Analyzed published research on the Tusk campaign.
5. Identified malicious domains, malware distribution infrastructure, and command-and-control servers.
6. Investigated malware configuration and cryptocurrency theft mechanisms.

## Key Findings
- Threat actors impersonated legitimate cryptocurrency platforms.
- Legitimate cloud storage services were abused to distribute malware.
- The campaign utilized information-stealing malware targeting sensitive information and cryptocurrency assets.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Indicator of Compromise (IOC) identification
- Hash analysis and intelligence pivoting
- Threat actor infrastructure analysis
- Malware research
- Threat intelligence report analysis

## Lessons Learned
This investigation strengthened my understanding of how cyber threat intelligence analysts transform individual indicators into actionable intelligence through research, correlation, and analysis.
